## What Works on YouTube Shorts for Dad Jokes (2026)

### Format Essentials

- **9:16 portrait, full-screen fill.** Keep all text and visuals in the central 75% of the frame — edges get cropped on some devices.
- **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** Viewers decide within 2 seconds whether to swipe. For joke content, show the setup text immediately — no long intros, no logo bumpers.
- **Optimal length: 20–35 seconds.** Two scripts per video at ~15 seconds each hits this window. Longer Shorts see drop-off before the punchline.
- **Captions on screen the entire time.** A large portion of Shorts are watched muted. Show every word as TTS speaks it (karaoke-style word highlight or full-line flash).

### Text Style (what's performing in 2026)

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Font | Bold, rounded sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat Bold, Poppins ExtraBold) |
| Size | SETUP/PUNCHLINE: 60–80pt equivalent. GROAN: large, exaggerated. CLOSER: slightly smaller |
| Color | White text on dark semi-transparent overlay, or black text on bright warm background |
| Animation | Fade-in per line (not per word for jokes — kills timing). Pop/scale on PUNCHLINE word |
| Groan line | Make it visually bigger, maybe a different color (e.g., red or orange) to signal the groan moment |

### Background Visuals — What Works for Faceless Dad Joke Channels

**Proven approaches seen on top-performing channels:**

1. **AI-generated lifestyle images** — A dad at a grill, in a recliner, mowing lawn, holding a coffee mug. Match the image to the joke's prop. AI images are now mainstream and perform as well as stock.
2. **Simple illustrated cartoon backgrounds** — A backyard patio, a living room, a garage workshop. Warm, not clinical.
3. **Solid color + texture** — A warm wood grain, kraft paper, or slightly textured warm beige/brown background. Clean and readable.
4. **Avoid:** Busy, distracting backgrounds. Generic office or city settings. Anything that doesn't read as "dad's world."

### Music

- **Soft acoustic guitar loop** or light jazzy ukulele — warm, dad-life feel.
- Keep music at 15–20% of TTS volume (TTS must be clearly audible).
- No copyright music. Use royalty-free sources: Pixabay Music, YouTube Audio Library, Uppbeat.
- Avoid anything upbeat-aggressive or trending/recognizable (it dates the content quickly).

### TTS Voice Setup

- Voice: `en-US-Standard-D` (deep, warm male voice) — already in template.
- Pace: Slightly slower than default (~0.9x) — dad joke timing benefits from deliberate delivery.
- Pause: Add a 0.5–0.8 second gap between PUNCHLINE and GROAN for comedic beat.
- GROAN: Can be delivered with a slight pitch drop for effect.

## Mild Bathroom Humor — Channel F Style Guide

Per updated content rules, mild gas/wind wordplay is permitted. Guidelines:

- **Allowed:** Wordplay involving "wind," "gas," "silent," "rip," "stink," "fumes," puns on words that sound like bodily functions.
- **Not allowed:** Descriptions of actual bodily fluids, gross-out imagery, excessive repetition of the theme.
- **Tone:** The joke should feel like a dad grinning at his own joke — not like shock humor.
- **Limit:** No more than 2 of 20 scripts per batch should use this theme (variety matters).

**Example mild bathroom humor script:**

```
[SETUP]
Dad says the dog is responsible for every mysterious smell in the house.
[PUNCHLINE]
We rehomed the dog two years ago.
[GROAN]
Still blaming the dog, Dad.
[CLOSER]
Happy Father's Day to every dad who keeps the alibi alive.
```
